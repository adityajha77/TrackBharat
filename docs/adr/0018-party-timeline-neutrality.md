# ADR-0018: Party timeline: one format, dated facts, free-licence photos

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-16

## Context
A "how India's parties evolved" page with leaders' photos, missions and milestones can easily become one-sided, and photos
of public figures are often copyrighted.

## Decision
- **Same structure for every party:** founding date, founders, a one-line neutral summary of how the party describes
  itself (with a link), symbol, 1–5 leading figures, dated milestones. Parties are ordered by founding date.
- **Facts, not adjectives:** each event is a date, a plain sentence and a source URL. Controversies, if added, follow the
  same rule for every party.
- **Quotes** appear only where we verified the words on the cited page (e.g. Tryst with Destiny, Garibi Hatao,
  Jai Jawan Jai Kisan / Jai Vigyan). The quote field is optional for everyone; no invented or "famous but unverified" quotes.
- **Photos:** fetched from Wikimedia Commons only when the file's licence is free (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA,
  GODL-India). Licence and author are stored and shown as a credit; the image is re-encoded into our own media (metadata
  stripped). Non-free (fair-use) images are skipped. MP photos from sansad.in are not used (that site forbids embedding).
- National milestones (Independence, Constitution, Emergency, liberalisation, 73rd Amendment, RTI…) are shared markers.

## Consequences
- ✅ The page teaches history without taking sides; every claim is checkable.
- ⚠️ Wikipedia is a secondary source; official sources replace it when available.
